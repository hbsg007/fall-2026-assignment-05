A kite shop run by people who fly

Crosswind is a family-run shop in Nags Head, started by two flyers who were tired of kites that broke on the first strong gust.

Our mission

Put a well-made kite in more hands, and keep it flying for years.

That means honest advice about wind, gear that’s worth repairing, and free lessons for anyone who walks down to the beach on a Saturday morning.

Our story

Maya Alvarez sewed her first delta from an old sailboat spinnaker when she was twelve. Years later, she and her brother Theo kept buying kites that tore or snapped within a weekend, so they started making their own in Theo’s garage, two blocks from the ocean.

A folding table at the beach access on Saturdays turned into a shop with a repair bench in the back. We still test-fly everything we sell, and we still fix small tears for free while you wait.

  1. 2014

    Maya and Theo sew their first ripstop deltas and sell them from a folding table on weekends.

  2. 2017

    The shop opens on Dune Street, with a repair bench in the back.

  3. 2020

    Free Saturday-morning flying lessons start on the beach.

  4. 2024

    The first Crosswind Fall Fly-In puts more than 200 kites in the air at once.

Visit or get in touch

We’re on Dune Street across from beach access 14, a short walk from the pier. Park out front and bring your kite straight down to the sand.
