Kites built for the wind you’ve got.

Crosswind Kite Works is a small kite shop on the Outer Banks of North Carolina. We sell, fix and fly single-line kites, stunt kites and the gear that keeps them in the air.

Shop the kite wall Read our story

Every kite on our wall gets flown on our beach first.

We started Crosswind in 2014 by sewing ripstop deltas in a garage two blocks from the ocean. Today we stock fifteen kites and pieces of gear we trust, and we’ll tell you honestly which one suits the wind where you fly.

Bring a broken kite by anytime. Our repair bench is open to everyone, wherever you bought it.

Featured categories

Not sure what to fly? Start with the wind.

Check the forecast for your beach, find the wind speed below, and shop from that part of the wall.

Browse all 15 items
  1. 4–10 mph Light breeze. Deltas and sled kites.
  2. 8–18 mph Steady wind. Diamonds, box kites and most kids’ kites.
  3. 15–25 mph Strong wind. Stunt kites and parafoils.
  4. Over 25 mph Too much. Leave the kites at home and watch the surf.
